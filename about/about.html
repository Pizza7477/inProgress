About The Website

What is inProgress?

Hi, I'm Pixxa, and this website is exactly what its name says: a work in progress.

I'm learning how to build websites, and inProgress is where I put each new lesson into practice. Every time I learn something new, whether it's a new layout trick, a bit of styling, or my first lines of JavaScript, it finds its way onto this site. Some pages will look rough, some things might break, and that's completely fine. That's how learning works.

Why "inProgress"?

I could have waited until I felt ready to build something polished, but I realised I'd probably be waiting forever. So instead I decided to build in the open, mistakes and all. This site is my notebook, my playground, and my record of how far I've come.

What I'm learning Right Now?

At the moment I'm working on the basics: HTML, CSS, and how to use Git and GitHub to keep track of my work. Next, I want to dive into JavaScript and learn how to make pages that actually respond to the people using them.

What to expect?

You'll probably notice the site changing from one visit to the next. Pages will get added, designs will get tweaked, and old experiments might disappear. If you come back in a few months, I hope you'll see a very different website, and a much better developer behind it.
